# Code of Conduct

## Principles
Vibestack is an engineering-focused open-source community. We prioritize respectful discourse, evidence-based decisions, and contributor growth.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and precise in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Document assumptions and constraints.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption or bad-faith review behavior.
